Bank Accounts

Savings Account

A savings account allows you to safely deposit money and earn interest on your balance.

Example Interest Rate: 4% per year

"Saving a little regularly can create a strong financial habit."

Current Account

Current accounts are generally designed for frequent transactions and business activities.

< id="cards">

Credit Cards

How Credit Cards Work

A credit card allows you to make purchases using a predefined credit limit.

Always understand the interest rate, annual charges and payment due date before using a credit card.

< class="tip"> Financial Tip:

Pay your credit card bill on time and avoid unnecessary debt.

< id="loans">

Understanding Loans

Personal Loan

A personal loan can be used for eligible personal expenses and is generally repaid through EMIs.

Home Loan

A home loan is used to finance the purchase or construction of an eligible residential property.

< class="formula"> EMI Formula

EMI = P × R × (1 + R)N ÷ ((1 + R)N − 1)

Important Financial Terms

Interest Rate
The percentage charged or earned on a principal amount.
EMI
Equated Monthly Instalment paid toward a loan.
Principal
The original amount borrowed or invested.
Tenure
The period for which a loan or financial product is active.
