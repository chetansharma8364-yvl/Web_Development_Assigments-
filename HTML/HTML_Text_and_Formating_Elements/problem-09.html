Why I Like Music

Music is something I listen to almost every day. I usually listen to music when I am travelling, studying, working, or just relaxing. It can easily change my mood and make a boring day a little better.

My Favourite Types of Music

I mostly enjoy pop, hip-hop, and alternative music. Different types of music work for different moods. Sometimes I want something energetic, while other times I prefer something calm and slow.

Music and Daily Life

I think music has become a normal part of our daily life. I always have a playlist ready when I am travelling or doing something that takes a lot of time. My favourite songs also remind me of certain moments and places.

My Favourite Thing About Music

The best thing about music is that everyone can have a different taste. A song that one person does not like can be another person's favourite. That is what makes music interesting.
