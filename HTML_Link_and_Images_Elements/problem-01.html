GLA University

About

GLA University is a private university located in Mathura, Uttar Pradesh. It provides students with opportunities to learn through academic programs, practical activities and a variety of campus facilities.

Programs

The university offers undergraduate and postgraduate programs in areas such as engineering, management, computer applications, science and commerce. Students can choose programs according to their interests and career goals.

Campus Life

Campus life at GLA University includes classrooms, laboratories, libraries, sports facilities and student activities. The campus provides an environment where students can study, participate in events and interact with other students.
