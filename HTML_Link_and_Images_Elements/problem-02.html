Major Technology Event Brings Students Together

College Hosts Annual Technology and Innovation Fest

A major technology event was organized at a college campus to bring students together and encourage creativity, innovation and teamwork. More than 500 students participated in the event, showcasing their ideas through different technology-based projects.

The event was held at the college campus in New Delhi on 1 September 2026. Students from different departments attended the program and presented projects related to software, artificial intelligence and modern technology.

The main purpose of the event was to give students an opportunity to apply their classroom knowledge to practical problems. The event also encouraged students to think creatively and develop innovative solutions for real-world challenges.

During the event, students interacted with teachers, guests and other participants. Several projects attracted attention because of their practical applications. The organizers said that such events help students improve their technical knowledge as well as communication and teamwork skills.

The college plans to organize more activities like this in the future so that students can continue developing their ideas and skills.
