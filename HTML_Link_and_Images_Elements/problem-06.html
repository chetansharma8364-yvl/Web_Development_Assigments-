TechFest 2026

College Technology Festival

Get ready for TechFest 2026, the biggest technology festival of our college! The event brings together students, technology enthusiasts and industry experts for an exciting experience filled with innovation, learning and creativity.

Introduction

TechFest 2026 is a fictional college technology festival designed to encourage students to explore modern technology and develop creative ideas. Participants will get opportunities to attend exciting activities, interact with experts and learn about new developments in the technology industry.

Highlights

The festival will feature technology demonstrations, coding challenges, project exhibitions and interactive sessions. Students can showcase their creativity and learn from other participants. The event will also provide an opportunity to discover new ideas and improve practical technical skills.

Date and Venue

Date: 15 October 2026
Time: 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM
Venue: College Main Auditorium
City: New Delhi, India

Registration deadline: 10 October 2026

Speakers

TechFest 2026 will feature experienced technology professionals, entrepreneurs and college faculty members as guest speakers. They will share their knowledge about software development, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity and future technologies.

The speaker sessions will help students understand how classroom concepts are applied in real-world technology projects and careers.

Registration

Students who are interested in participating can register online before the deadline. Registration is open to students who want to attend the sessions, participate in activities or showcase their technology projects.
