University Admission Handbook

Admission Overview

The University Admission Handbook provides important information about the admission process, eligibility requirements, documents, important terms, fees and contact details.

Students are advised to complete every admission step carefully and submit all required documents before the deadline.

Eligibility

Students must meet the eligibility requirements of the selected course. A valid qualifying examination certificate is mandatory.

Applicants should check the required academic qualifications before starting the application process.

Application Process

  1. Visit the official university admission website.
  2. Read the admission requirements carefully.
  3. Complete the application procedure.
  4. Upload or submit the required documents.
  5. Pay the application fee.
  6. Wait for the admission decision and complete registration.

Application Deadline: 30 September 2026

Required Documents

  • Class 10 Marksheet
  • Class 12 Marksheet
  • Transfer Certificate
  • Migration Certificate
  • Passport Size Photograph
  • Government Identity Proof

Important Terms

Eligibility
The conditions that a student must satisfy to apply for a course.
Merit List
A list of students selected according to their admission performance.
Counselling
The process of selecting a course or seat based on availability and eligibility.
Scholarship
Financial assistance provided to eligible students.
Registration
The process of officially enrolling in the university.
