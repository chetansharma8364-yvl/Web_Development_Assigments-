Spice Garden Restaurant

Welcome to Spice Garden Restaurant, where we serve fresh and delicious food prepared with quality ingredients. Enjoy a variety of starters, main courses, desserts and beverages.

Starters

  • Spring Rolls - Crispy rolls filled with vegetables
  • Veg Manchurian - Vegetable balls in spicy sauce

Main Course

  • Paneer Butter Masala - Paneer cooked in creamy tomato gravy
  • Veg Biryani - Aromatic rice cooked with vegetables and spices
  • Dal Makhani - Slow-cooked black lentils with butter
  • Masala Dosa - Crispy dosa served with potato filling

Desserts

  • Gulab Jamun - Soft sweet dumplings in sugar syrup
  • Chocolate Brownie - Warm chocolate brownie
  • Ice Cream - Choice of vanilla, chocolate or strawberry

Beverages

  • Fresh Lime Soda
  • Masala Chai
  • Cold Coffee
