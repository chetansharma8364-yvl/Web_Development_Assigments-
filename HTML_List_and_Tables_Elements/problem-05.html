Annual College Fest 2026

Welcome to Annual College Fest 2026, a three-day celebration of talent, creativity, competition and entertainment. Students from different departments can participate in various cultural, technical and sports events.

Fest Introduction

The Annual College Fest will bring students together for exciting competitions, performances and activities. The fest will be held over three days at different venues across the college campus.

Registration Process

  1. Choose the competition you want to participate in.
  2. Read the eligibility and competition rules.
  3. Complete the registration process.
  4. Submit the required participant details.
  5. Pay the registration fee if applicable.
  6. Save your registration reference for future use.

Fest Rules

  • Students must carry their valid college ID cards.
  • Participants must report at the venue before the scheduled time.
  • Participants must follow instructions given by coordinators.
  • Any form of cheating or unfair practice is strictly prohibited.
  • Participants must maintain cleanliness around the campus.
  • The decision of the event judges will be final.

Important Fest Terms

Open Event
An event in which individual students from different colleges can participate.
Team Event
An event where participants compete as a group or team.
Individual Event
An event where one participant competes independently.
Registration
The process of officially enrolling for a fest event.
Coordinator
A person responsible for organizing and managing an event.
Final Round
The last stage of a competition where finalists compete for the prizes.
