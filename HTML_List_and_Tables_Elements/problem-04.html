Laptop Comparison Guide

Choosing the right laptop depends on your studies, budget and daily requirements. This guide compares popular laptops based on their performance, memory, storage, display, battery and warranty.

How to Choose Your Laptop

  1. Decide your budget.
  2. Choose a suitable processor for your work.
  3. Check the amount of RAM and storage.
  4. Compare display quality and battery life.
  5. Check the warranty and after-sales service.
  6. Compare the final prices and choose the best option.

Important Technical Terms

RAM
Random Access Memory temporarily stores data used by running applications.
SSD
Solid State Drive is a fast storage device used to store files and programs.
CPU
Central Processing Unit performs the main calculations and instructions of a computer.
GPU
Graphics Processing Unit handles graphics and visual processing.
Refresh Rate
The number of times the display updates its image per second, measured in Hz.
Battery Capacity
The amount of energy a laptop battery can store, commonly measured in Wh.
