Important Medical Terms

Consultation
Initial checkup and discussion with a doctor regarding your health.
Follow-up
A return visit to review test results or check recovery progress.
OPD
Outpatient Department for treatment without overnight stay.
Appointment
A scheduled time slot reserved for a patient meeting.
