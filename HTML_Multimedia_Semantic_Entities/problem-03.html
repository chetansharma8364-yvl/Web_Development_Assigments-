Travel Tips

  • Carry a valid passport and travel documents.
  • Use public transport for convenient travel.
  • Keep your belongings secure in crowded places.
  • Book popular attractions in advance.
  • Learn a few basic French phrases.

One-Day Itinerary

  1. Visit the Eiffel Tower.
  2. Explore the Louvre Museum.
  3. Have lunch near the Seine.
  4. Visit Notre-Dame.
  5. Enjoy an evening walk around Montmartre.
