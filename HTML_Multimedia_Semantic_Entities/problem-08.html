Bank Accounts

Savings Account

A savings account allows individuals to safely deposit money and may provide interest on the balance.

  • Suitable for personal savings.
  • May provide interest.
  • Allows deposits and withdrawals.
  • May include a debit card.
  • Online banking may be available.
Example Interest Rate
4% p.a.
Minimum Balance
As specified by the bank

Current Account

A current account is commonly used by businesses and customers who require frequent transactions.

  • Suitable for businesses.
  • Supports frequent transactions.
  • May provide higher transaction limits.
  • Business banking facilities may be available.

Bank Cards

Debit Card

A debit card allows customers to make payments using money available in their bank account.

  • Connected to a bank account.
  • Can be used at ATMs.
  • Can be used for online purchases.
  • Can be used at shops.

Credit Card

A credit card provides a credit limit that can be used for eligible purchases and repaid according to the card agreement.

  • Provides a credit limit.
  • Has a billing cycle.
  • May provide reward points.
  • Interest may apply to unpaid balances.
Example Credit Limit
₹1,00,000
Example Annual Charge
₹999 + applicable taxes

Responsible Card Usage

  1. Pay the bill before the due date.
  2. Keep credit usage under control.
  3. Avoid unnecessary purchases.
  4. Check monthly statements.
  5. Report suspicious transactions.

Responsible Personal Finance

Creating a Monthly Budget

A monthly budget helps you understand income and expenses and plan your money effectively.

  • Record monthly income.
  • List essential expenses.
  • Track unnecessary spending.
  • Set a monthly saving target.
  • Review your budget regularly.

Emergency Fund

An emergency fund can help cover unexpected expenses such as repairs, emergencies or temporary income loss.

A suitable emergency fund depends on your income, expenses and personal circumstances.

Debt Management

  1. Understand the interest rate.
  2. Check all applicable charges.
  3. Know the monthly EMI.
  4. Understand the loan tenure.
  5. Pay EMIs on time.
  6. Avoid unnecessary borrowing.

Financial Security

  • Never share your PIN.
  • Never share your OTP.
  • Use strong passwords.
  • Enable transaction alerts.
  • Use secure websites.
  • Report suspicious activity.

Important Financial Terms

Principal

Principal is the original amount deposited, invested or borrowed before interest.

Interest Rate

Interest rate is the percentage applied to a principal amount for a specific period.

Example: 8% p.a.

EMI

EMI means Equated Monthly Instalment. It is a regular payment made towards a loan.

Tenure

Tenure is the period for which a loan or financial product remains active.

Example: 60 months

Annual Percentage Rate

APR may represent the annual cost of borrowing and may include certain applicable charges.

Banking Notices and Safety

Online Banking Safety

Never share passwords, PINs, OTPs or other authentication information with anyone.

Transaction Monitoring

Check your account statements and transaction alerts regularly for unfamiliar activity.

Fraud Awareness

Be careful of unexpected calls, messages or emails asking for banking credentials or money.

Safety Reminder

Banks and legitimate financial organizations should not require you to disclose your confidential authentication information to unknown callers.
